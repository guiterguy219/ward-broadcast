# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Members of the Payson 10th Ward Elders Quorum and Relief Society, ages 18 to 90+. Most arrive on a phone, from a link in a text, the ward bulletin, or a presidency member at church, often while standing in a hallway or at home on the couch. Many are older and not technical. Their one job is to sign up for (or understand) ward announcement texts, in under a minute, without feeling like they're joining a marketing list.

A second, smaller audience is carrier and Telnyx reviewers checking that the opt-in, terms, and privacy pages are clear and compliant.

## Product Purpose
A volunteer-run, opt-in text list so the quorum and Relief Society presidencies can reach everyone with a phone, not just the 60% who install a group app. The site has four surfaces: a short home page, the signup form, terms, and privacy. Success is a member signing up confidently on the first try and trusting what they agreed to.

## Brand Personality
Warm, neighborly, trustworthy. It should read like a well-made ward bulletin handed to you by a friend: plain-spoken, calm, and quietly cared-for. Copy is short and direct, uses "you" and "we," and never sounds like it's selling something.

## Anti-references
- Marketing or SaaS landing pages: heroes, gradients, startup polish, feature grids.
- Official Church branding: nothing that imitates ChurchofJesusChrist.org's look, wordmark, or symbol (General Handbook 38.8.9 and 38.8.21.2).
- Spammy SMS-signup forms: coupon-club, "text DEALS to 12345," urgency badges.
- Dated church websites: clip art, centered serif headers on beige, 2008-era layouts.

## Design Principles
- **Trust before everything.** The consent language is the product; make it easy to read, not fine print.
- **One obvious next step.** Each page has a single clear action; nothing competes with it.
- **Neighborly, not institutional.** Feels like the ward, not like the Church or a company.
- **Readable by everyone.** Designed first for an older member on an older phone.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Base text at least 17px with generous line height; tap targets at least 44px; strong contrast in both light and dark modes; clear focus states; form errors in plain words next to the fields they concern; respects reduced motion. No information conveyed by color alone.
